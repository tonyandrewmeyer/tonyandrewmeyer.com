Last time: [Benjer Nectarine](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/13/benjer-nectarine/), this time: [Benjer Mango & Apple](https://benjer.co.nz/product/benjer-mango-apple/).

275mL glass bottle. Ordered online - I hope I see these out and about in the future.

Outstanding. Fresh mango taste, although I think I would like slightly more mango and slightly less apple (as is typical for a juice, the apple isn't there to be part of the taste). All the excellent qualities of their nectarine juice, but a bit less thick and, probably as a result, I feel I could drink several of these in a row. Would probably be even better in the summer, but still refreshing on a moderately hot winter day.

Overall: 9.1/10.
