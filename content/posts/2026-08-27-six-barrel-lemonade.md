Last time: [Skinny Fizz Raspberry](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/27/skinny-fizz-raspberry/), this time: [Six Barrel Lemonade](https://www.sixbarrelsoda.co/products/lemonade-15x330ml).

330mL can, ordered online. I don't think I've seen these anywhere else.

There's a distinctive Six Barrel taste that runs through everything of theirs I've had, and, unfortunately, I'm not much of a fan. When I had the [Ginger Ale](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/05/six-barrel-ginger-ale/) I put my fairly lukewarm response down to not being a ginger ale person, which was true enough, but with a lemonade in front of me that can't be the only issue. It's the house style I don't enjoy, not the flavour it's wrapped around.

Underneath that, this is a regular lemonade. Nothing wrong with it and nothing surprising in it, which is the problem: if the drink underneath were doing something interesting I'd be willing to put up with a taste I don't enjoy, but ordinary lemonade plus a taste I don't enjoy doesn't leave much for me to like.

Not one I'd order again, although I suspect that says more about me than it does about the drink. 4.8/10.
