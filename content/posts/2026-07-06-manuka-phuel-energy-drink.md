Last time: [Barrier Buzz Ginger Beer](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/06/barrier-buzz-ginger-beer/), this time [Mānuka Phuel Big Buzz Energy Drink](https://shop.mphuel.co.nz/products/big-buzz-energy-drink).

A 250ml can, picked up at New World - I haven't seen it anywhere else.

Interestingly, this tastes of honey (slightly), and of what fuel - petrol - smells like it would taste like. So the name is pretty accurate, but that's not what I'm looking for in a drink, even an energy drink. It might appeal to some, but not to me. I do hope someone else has a go at a honey-forward energy drink, because it's a great idea - just not this execution.

2.5/10.
