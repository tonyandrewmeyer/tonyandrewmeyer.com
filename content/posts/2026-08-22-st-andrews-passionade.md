Last time: [Picky Apple & Feijoa](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/15/picky-apple-and-feijoa/), this time: [St Andrews Limes Passionade](https://limes.co.nz/products/passionade-sparkling-soda).

330mL can, bought from New World, although I think I've seen these in other places too.

There's nothing technically wrong with this. It's real New Zealand fruit juice, it's made properly, and maybe the people who like passionfruit would be happy. I am not one of those people. I don't generally drink or eat passionfruit, and when I do it's probably only as part of some sort of mix - so a drink that puts it front and centre was never going to go well for me. The lime doesn't help either: it takes an already-assertive flavour and makes it significantly sour.

I could barely finish this one, and it's not one I'll be having again.

1/10.
