Last time: [Good Sh*t Ginger](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/23/good-shit-ginger/), this time: [Benjer Raspberry & Apple](https://benjer.co.nz/product/benjer-raspberry-apple/).

275mL glass bottle, ordered online.

This is very close to the [Boysenberry & Apple](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/23/benjer-boysenberry-and-apple/), both in taste and in the conclusion I came to: it turns out that I would rather eat a raspberry than drink one.

A perfectly reasonable juice, but there are others I would have before it - including several other Benjer ones.

5.9/10.
