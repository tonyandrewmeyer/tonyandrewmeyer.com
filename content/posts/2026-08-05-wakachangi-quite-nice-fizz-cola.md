Last time: [Foxton Fizz, Lime](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/05/foxton-fizz-lime/), this time: [Wakachangi Quite Nice Fizz Cola](https://www.newworld.co.nz/shop/product/5348693_ea_000nw?name=wakachangi-quite-nice-fizz-cola-soft-drink).

330mL can, from New World.

I knew about the beer, and I've tried several of the chips, but I had no idea Wakachangi did soft drinks as well - I think this might be the first one.

It's interesting. This is definitely the 'classic' cola taste rather than a Coke taste, which is the distinction I keep running into: my favourite soft drink is Coke, and things that aren't trying to be Coke tend to lose me. This one doesn't lose me, though. It's not bad at all.

I don't think I'd reach for it, but I wouldn't complain about being given one.

5.6/10.
