Last time: [St Andrews Limes Limonade](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/09/06/st-andrews-limonade/), this time: [Skinny Fizz Lemon](https://skinnyfizz.co.nz/products/lemon).

330mL can, bought online, same as the [raspberry](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/27/skinny-fizz-raspberry/) and the [lime](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/29/skinny-fizz-lime/).

This tastes much like the other two, which is to say a strong sparkling water taste with a hint of lemon behind it. If I liked sparkling water then I suspect I would like these, but I don't, so I don't. Neither half of it works for me: not the base, and not the lemon on top of it.

4.4/10.
