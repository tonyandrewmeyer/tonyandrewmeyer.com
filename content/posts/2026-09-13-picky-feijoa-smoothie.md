Last time: [Barrier Buzz Cola](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/09/13/barrier-buzz-cola/), this time: [Picky Feijoa Smoothie](https://wearepicky.co.nz/products/picky-feijoa-smoothie).

800mL plastic bottle, the same as the rest of the Picky juices and smoothies.

The smoothies are as good as the juices, which is to say: great. The [sparkling waters](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/29/picky-hydrate-lemon-and-lime/) are another story.

There's apple in here as well, but where the [Apple & Feijoa](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/15/picky-apple-and-feijoa/) juice tastes of both, this is a pure feijoa taste, and much thicker for being a smoothie. Excellent.

9.3/10.
