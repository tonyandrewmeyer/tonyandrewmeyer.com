Last time: [NZ Juice Wildberries](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/23/nz-juice-wildberries/), this time: [Good Juju Pre+ Pro+ Postbiotic Raspberry Soda](https://www.goodjujusoda.com/products/raspberry-soda).

330mL can, and a delightfully bright pink one at that, found in a Wellington supermarket - like the [lemonade](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/09/good-juju-lemonade/), I haven't seen it anywhere else.

My expectations were very low, based on that first Good Juju experience. I was very pleasantly surprised. This has a very nice raspberry flavour, and while there's a hint at the end of what I assume is the pre- and post-biotic taste, it's pretty mild - nothing like the lingering disgust of the lemonade.

Raspberry isn't what I'd generally pick as a drinking fruit, but here it's quite good. I'm not sure I would seek this out, but I would happily drink it again - perhaps in some work-themed way ;).

7.1/10.
