Last time: [Clean Collective Energy Pineapple](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/10/03/clean-collective-energy-pineapple/), this time: [Lewis Road Creamery Caramel and Butterscotch](https://lewisroadcreamery.co.nz/products/caramel-butterscotch).

300ml plastic bottle, bought at a supermarket, although of course these are pretty much everywhere. Last time I [wondered](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/09/28/lewis-road-lime-milkshake/) why anyone would buy the 300ml size, and it turns out the answer is that this flavour doesn't come in a 750ml bottle.

This is my least favourite of the Lewis Road range, but I think that's mostly down to me rather than the drink: I'm not a big fan of either butterscotch or caramel. The thing Lewis Road is really good at is getting the authentic taste of the flavour into a milk, and they've done that here too, so if I liked either of those, I expect this would be a winner. 5.2/10.
