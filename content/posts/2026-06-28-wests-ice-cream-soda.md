Last time: [Optimum Nutrition Essential Amin.O. Energy Blueberry Lemonade](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/06/28/optimum-nutrition-amino-energy-blueberry-lemonade/), this time [Wests Ice Cream Soda](https://wests.co.nz/products/ice-cream-soda).

350ml plastic bottle, disappointingly. I like creaming soda a lot as a flavour, and I love ice cream sodas, so I came into this with high expectations.

This is meant to be fizzy - it's "ice cream soda" flavoured! - and carbonated water is even listed as an ingredient, but it was flat, with maybe the slightest hint of bubbles. Maybe I had a bad bottle? I checked and it was not expired. I ordered online, so maybe it didn't survive the trip well - I haven't seen it for sale anywhere else.

I'd have it once more to verify, but if it tasted the same: never again. 2.5/10.
