Last time: [NZ Natural Juice Feijoa](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/31/nz-juice-feijoa/), this time: [Six Barrel Cola Six](https://www.sixbarrelsoda.co/products/cola-six-330ml).

330mL can, ordered online, same as the rest of the Six Barrel range.

The name is the six flavours that go into it: lime, orange, cinnamon, kola nut, coriander seed and vanilla. That list tells you exactly what sort of cola this is going to be, and it's the sort I like least - the citrus-and-spice craft cola, as far from Coke as a cola can get while still being called one. I've said before that what I actually like is Coke and things adjacent to it, and this is at the opposite end.

On top of that there's the [distinctive Six Barrel taste](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/27/six-barrel-lemonade/) that runs through everything of theirs I've had and that I've never got on with. So this is the house style I don't enjoy wrapped around the cola style I don't enjoy, which is a lose-lose, and the result is exactly what you'd predict: something I would rather skip.

It's not undrinkable. 4.4/10.
