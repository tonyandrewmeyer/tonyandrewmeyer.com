Last time: [Karma Drinks Organic Gingerella](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/29/karma-drinks-gingerella/), this time: [Pete's Natural Feijoa Lemonade](https://petesnatural.co.nz/shop/feijoa-lemonade-12-pack-cans/).

250mL can, and I think this one was a gift from my sister. I've ordered Pete's online before, but I'm not sure that I've seen these anywhere else - but I did find the [plain lemonade](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/06/14/petes-natural-lemonade/) at New World so maybe there?

This is nice, and the feijoa is distinct. It's only part of it, though, rather than the full feijoa flavour - perhaps the lemonade half overpowers it?

I'd have it again, but it's not the best of the feijoa drinks: the [Aroha Sparkling Feijoa](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/04/02/aroha-sparkling-feijoa/), the [Benjer Feijoa & Apple](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/15/benjer-feijoa-and-apple/) and the [Picky Apple & Feijoa](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/15/picky-apple-and-feijoa/) are all ahead of it.

6.7/10.
