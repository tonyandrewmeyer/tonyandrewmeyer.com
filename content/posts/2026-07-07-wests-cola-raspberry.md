Last time: [Mānuka Phuel Big Buzz Energy Drink](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/06/manuka-phuel-energy-drink/), this time [Wests Cola & Raspberry](https://wests.co.nz/products/cola-raspberry).

350ml plastic bottle, as usual for the Wests range. Ordered online - I haven't seen it anywhere else.

Tastes pretty similar to a raspberry coke. Finally some fizz, although still not a lot of it. I'm still unsure if the delivery process has caused the lack of quality, or if it's something else going on with the Wests range.

Adequate, but not something I would reach for. 5/10.
