Last time: [Benjer Boysenberry & Apple](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/23/benjer-boysenberry-and-apple/), this time: [Good Sh*t Ginger](https://lovegoodshit.com/products/good-sh-t-ginger).

330mL can, from a supermarket - New World, I think, although I wouldn't swear to it.

This is more ginger ale than ginger beer, which is unfortunate since I prefer the latter. Apparently, there are three other flavours, all of which I think I would prefer, so I should try thise at some point.

It's probably good for me, and I suspect that's rather the point. But being good for me isn't quite enough on its own: the taste is fine rather than enjoyable, and there's nothing here that would have me reaching for another one.

4.3/10.
