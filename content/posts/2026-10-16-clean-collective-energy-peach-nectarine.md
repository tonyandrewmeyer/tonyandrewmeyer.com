Last time: [Boring Break Chocolate](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/10/16/boring-break-chocolate/), this time: [Clean Collective Energy Peach and Nectarine](https://cleancollective.co.nz/products/energy-peach-nectarine).

330ml can, bought at New World, although you can also get it online.

This is very similar to the [pineapple](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/10/03/clean-collective-energy-pineapple/) and [forest berry](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/10/03/clean-collective-energy-forest-berry/) ones, but (obviously) peach and nectarine flavoured. I get more peach flavour than nectarine, which is unfortunate since I prefer nectarine, although neither is particularly high on my list of fruit.

I didn't notice this with the other two (and I don't have the cans any more to check), but neither peach nor nectarine is in the ingredients list. That's a bit surprising given how strong and realistic the taste is, and given that this seems to be aiming to be the healthy choice.

I'd pick either of the other two flavours over this, and it's still a sparkling water (or an energy water?), so it's not my first pick, but it's OK. 5.3/10.
