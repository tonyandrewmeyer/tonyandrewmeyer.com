Last time: [Karma Drinks Organic Lemmy Lemonade](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/22/karma-drinks-lemmy-lemonade/), this time: [Benjer Orange](https://benjer.co.nz/product/benjer-orange/).

275mL glass bottle. Ordered online - I still don't see these anywhere else, unfortunately.

A good, solid orange juice. It's more tart than [Keri](https://www.coca-cola.com/nz/en/brands/keri/products/juice-kitchen), which remains my favourite, but it's non-pulpy, which is very much my preference, and it makes for a properly refreshing cold drink.

6.2/10.
