Last time: [Good Juju Pre+ Pro+ Postbiotic Raspberry Soda](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/25/good-juju-raspberry/), this time: [Skinny Fizz Raspberry](https://skinnyfizz.co.nz/products/raspberry).

330mL can, bought online. I have a feeling I've seen these somewhere else as well, but I can't think of where.

This is a soda water with a splash of flavour, not a raspberry soft drink. Despite the impression recent posts might give, I drink a lot of water, and I like it. I also drink plenty of soft drink, and like that. But sparkling water is the one I've never been able to get on board with, and adding a bit of flavour doesn't change that. This is better than plain soda water, and the raspberry that's there is a pretty decent taste. It's just not my thing.

4.9/10.
