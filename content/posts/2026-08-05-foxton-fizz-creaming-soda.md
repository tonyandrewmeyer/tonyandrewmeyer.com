Last time: [Picky Tropical Smoothie](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/05/picky-tropical-smoothie/), this time: [Foxton Fizz, Creaming Soda](https://foxtonfizz.com/products/creaming-soda).

250mL glass bottle, available online direct and at New World.

I didn't know until recently that I liked creaming soda. I think I thought of it as an old person's drink, although I'm not sure why. And maybe I am old now. But it makes no sense at all, in hindsight: I like an ice cream soda, and I like a dirty soda, and the taste is not far off either of those.

This has all the Foxton attributes I love - the glass bottle, the nostalgia, the general sense that someone cared about getting it right - and it's a genuinely delicious flavour that I don't come across often.

Very glad I got over myself on this one. 9.2/10.
