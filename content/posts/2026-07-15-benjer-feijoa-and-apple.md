Last time: [Benjer Mango & Apple](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/13/benjer-mango-and-apple/), this time: [Benjer Feijoa & Apple](https://benjer.co.nz/product/benjer-feijoa-apple/).

275mL glass bottle. Ordered online - I haven't seen them elsewhere yet, but hopefully that changes in the future.

Feijoa are such a fascinating fruit - not native to NZ, and not only found here, but practically our native fruit anyway. If you haven't already, you should read [Kate Evans' Feijoa Book](https://kateevans.org/feijoa). I'm not a huge fan of eating feijoa, but I do love the taste and drinking it, and it pairs very well with other fruits. This one is strongly feijoa, with only a small taste of apple - just enough to make it taste like a juice and not a feijoa slushie. I think it would be a good introduction to feijoa as a taste. I wonder if I can take juice overseas - it's getting increasingly difficult to take small gifts for colleagues when travelling. I'd definitely have this again, particularly out of feijoa season when they aren't everywhere you look.

Overall: 9.4/10.
