Last time: [Good Morning Juice Orange, Apple, and Mango](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/09/good-morning-juice-orange-apple-mango/), this time: [Benjer Apricot](https://benjer.co.nz/product/benjer-apricot/).

275mL glass bottle. Ordered online - I have never seen these anywhere else. There was a slight mix-up with the order: I paid at checkout, but then got an invoice emailed the next day and paid it, assuming the original payment hadn't gone through. I wasn't actually meant to get the invoice, and ended up paying twice - but Benjer phoned me, apologised, and sorted out a refund, so no real issue.

Quite a thick, viscous juice, in a pleasant way that gives it a quality feel. Strong apricot flavour - I think I would really like this, except that I don't really like apricot. Refreshing, cold from the fridge.

Overall: 6.7/10.
