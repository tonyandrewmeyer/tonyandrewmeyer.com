Last time: [Coes Crafted Cola](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/29/coes-crafted-cola/), this time: [Six Barrel Ginger Ale](https://www.sixbarrelsoda.co/products/ginger-ale-15-x-330ml).

330mL can, ordered online. I don't think I've seen these anywhere else, although I wouldn't be surprised to find out otherwise.

Taste-wise it's very much of a piece with the other Six Barrel sodas I've had: the same house style, well made, clearly the work of people who know what they're doing.

The catch is that I'm not much of a ginger ale person to begin with. This is a perfectly good example of the thing; the thing just isn't really my thing. If ginger ale were higher on my list, I suspect I'd rate this a good deal higher.

5.1/10.
