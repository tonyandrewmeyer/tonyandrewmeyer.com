Last time: [Lewis Road Creamery Lime Milkshake](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/09/28/lewis-road-lime-milkshake/), this time: [Homegrown Feijoa Smoothie](https://www.homegrownjuice.co.nz/raw-cold-pasteurised-range.html) (with raw apple juice).

400ml plastic bottle, from the chilled juice section at the supermarket (at least Woolworths and New World).

This is good, as you'd expect from a feijoa and apple juice mix. It's not as good as some of the others, though ([Benjer](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/15/benjer-feijoa-and-apple/), [NZ Juice](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/31/nz-juice-feijoa/), and both the Picky [juice](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/15/picky-apple-and-feijoa/) and [smoothie](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/09/13/picky-feijoa-smoothie/)). Somehow the feijoa part isn't quite what I'm after, and it's also grassy, in a way.

The label says it's "cold pressed", "cold pasteurised", and "raw". I'm not really sure what those give you. Maybe this is extra healthy or something, or maybe that's part of why it tastes good at all.

I'd have this again, but I'd choose one of those other juices over it. 6.6/10.
