Last time: [Skinny Fizz Lemon](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/09/06/skinny-fizz-lemon/), this time: [Barrier Buzz Cola](https://theaoteacompany.co.nz/products/barrier-buzz-cola).

A 300ml glass bottle, the same as the [lemonade](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/06/07/barrier-buzz-lemonade/) and the [ginger beer](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/06/barrier-buzz-ginger-beer/). Bought online, and I still haven't seen these for sale anywhere else.

The cola taste is good, and the honey taste is the usual Barrier Buzz honey taste, which is also good. The problem is that the two of them together don't work that well: the honey-and-cola combination isn't a great one, and this is the weakest of their flavours because of it.

5.4/10.
