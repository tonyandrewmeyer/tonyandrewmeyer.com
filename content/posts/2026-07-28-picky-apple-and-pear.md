Last time: [NZ Juice Orange](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/27/nz-juice-orange/), this time: [Picky Apple & Pear](https://wearepicky.co.nz/products/picky-apple-pear-juice).

800mL plastic bottle (great size, disappointing material). Available from both New World and Woolworths (still Countdown, in my head), but from the chilled section, so presumably not something you can order online, at least rurally.

Apple and pear is a delightful combination, and this is a very well done mix. It's only 10% pear, but the pear comes through very clearly. I wish they specified the apple and pear varieties, but - like most - they don't. Delicious cold from the fridge, and great over ice.

Delicious. 8.8/10.
