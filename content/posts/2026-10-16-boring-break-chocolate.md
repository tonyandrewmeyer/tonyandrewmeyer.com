Last time: [Homegrown 100% Pure Orange Juice Low Pulp](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/10/04/homegrown-pure-orange-low-pulp/), this time: [Boring Break Chocolate](https://boringmilk.com/products/boring%C2%AE-break-chocolate-6-x-1l-bottles).

300ml plastic bottle, bought at a Z service station - I haven't seen it anywhere else, although you can get it online direct (by the dozen).

Not bad. You can tell that it's oat and not real milk, but it's still clearly going for the "chocolate milk" flavour, and it gets there. The chocolate is fairly mild - this certainly isn't trying to be a Pūhoi or a [Lewis Road](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/10/03/lewis-road-chocolate-milk/).

If I was wanting to avoid real milk, I could see myself drinking this again - it's a lot closer to the real thing than a coconut chocolate "milk", for example. Given that I'm not, though, probably not. 6.4/10.
