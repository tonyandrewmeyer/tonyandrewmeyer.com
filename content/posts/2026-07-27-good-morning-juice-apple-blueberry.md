Last time: [Wests Pineapple & Pear](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/27/wests-pineapple-and-pear/), this time: [Good Morning Juice Apple & Blueberry](https://www.nzjuice.co.nz/collections/good-morning-juice/products/good-morning-juice-apple-blueberry-single-bottle).

2.0L plastic bottle, ordered online - the same as the [orange, apple, and mango](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/09/good-morning-juice-orange-apple-mango/) from a few weeks back. I've never seen this anywhere else, in any size.

Perfectly reasonable juice, and I have no complaints about how it's made. The problem is entirely a me-and-blueberry problem: I'm not a big fan of blueberry as a juice, and here it thoroughly dominates the apple.

Blueberry as the raw fruit is fine, but in small quantities. I think it's at its best as a small addition to something else - a handful in a muffin or loaf, or scattered on top of a dessert - rather than as the dominant flavour of anything. A two-litre bottle of it is very much the opposite of a small addition, even with the apple base.

5.1/10.
