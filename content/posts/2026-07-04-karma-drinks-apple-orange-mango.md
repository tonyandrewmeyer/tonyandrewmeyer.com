Last time: [Wests Raspberry](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/04/wests-raspberry/), this time: [Karma Drinks Apple, Orange, and Mango Juice](https://karmadrinks.co.nz/products/karma-orange-apple-mango-juice).

300ml glass bottle, as with the rest of the Karma juice range. The usual Karma availability applies: online direct, supermarkets, and cafés.

Interestingly, this one is labelled "organic", while the [orange juice](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/04/karma-drinks-orange-juice/) is "natural". Taste-wise, it's a fairly standard tropical blend - the mango does most of the heavy lifting, with the apple and orange rounding it out rather than standing on their own. It's a nice juice, definitely not the best juice ever, but perfectly enjoyable.

You can trust Karam to be at least good, so always a safe choice. Overall: 6/10.
