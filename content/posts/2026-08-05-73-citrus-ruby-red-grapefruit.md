Last time: [Foxton Fizz, Orange](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/05/foxton-fizz-orange/), this time: ['73 Citrus, Ruby Red Grapefruit](https://www.newworld.co.nz/shop/product/5314887-ea-000?name=73-citrus-ruby-red-grapefruit-carbonated-drinks).

330mL can, from New World, I'm fairly sure.

I've never been a huge fan of grapefruit, although I do like it in small doses in juice form. This is exactly what you'd expect from the name: grapefruit, but with fizz. No surprises, no twist, nothing hiding behind it.

The trouble is the quantity. A 100mL taste of this would be great - bright, bitter, properly grapefruit-y. A whole 330mL can is just too much grapefruit for me, and by the end I was working through it rather than enjoying it.

Nothing wrong with the drink as such. It's doing precisely what it set out to do; I'm just not the person it's doing it for. I see they do an orange; I have never seen it on a shelf, but it would be interesting based on this.

4.7/10.
