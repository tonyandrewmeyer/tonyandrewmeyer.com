Last time: [Hakanoa Dry Ginger Beer](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/04/17/hakanoa-dry-ginger-beer/), this time [Wests Fruit Cocktail](https://wests.co.nz/products/fruit-cocktail).

This comes in a plastic bottle, unfortunately, but a reasonable size, 350ml. I've never seen or heard of this before doing some random searches for drinks online. As usual, no issues with having rural post deliver some, though.

I believe the flavour is meant to be some sort of generic fruit punch. It tasted to me a bit like a nicer Fanta (Orange), but not so much that I'm in a hurry to try it again. I forgot to try it with ice, but it was fine just fridge-chilled.

I doubt I would have this again, unless there wasn't much else on offer. 5.1/10.
