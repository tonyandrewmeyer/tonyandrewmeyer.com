Last time: [Skinny Fizz Lime](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/29/skinny-fizz-lime/), this time: [Picky Hydrate Lemon & Lime](https://wearepicky.co.nz/products/lemon-lime).

250mL can, my third in the Hydrate range after the [Berry](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/08/picky-hydrate-berry/) and the [Orange & Mango](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/10/picky-hydrate-orange-and-mango/).

Picky make great juices - the [Apple & Feijoa](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/15/picky-apple-and-feijoa/) is one of my favourites - so I keep hoping their will be a sparkling water I enjoy. I'm not a sparkling water person so it's a challenge. But this is definitely not it: the flavour itself is unappealing.

2.0/10.
