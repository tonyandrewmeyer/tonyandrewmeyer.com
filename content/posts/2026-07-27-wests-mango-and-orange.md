Last time: [Benjer Feijoa & Apple](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/15/benjer-feijoa-and-apple/), this time: [Wests Mango & Orange](https://wests.co.nz/products/mango-orange).

600mL plastic bottle. Ordered online - I haven't seen it anywhere else.

Finally, a Wests that has fizz! Not a lot of fizz, but at least it wasn't flat. This is also a 600mL bottle, which is much larger than the other Wests I've tried - I wonder if that's related to the fizz/no-fizz issue?

Otherwise, it's a pretty generic orange-and-mango soft drink. Not amazing, but perfectly fine. I'd have this if there wasn't anything else available, but I wouldn't seek it out.

5.8/10.
