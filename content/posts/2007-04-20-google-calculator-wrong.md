[![Google’s calculation](/uploads/2007/04/google_calories_to_joules.png)](/uploads/2007/04/google_calories_to_joules.png "Google’s calculation")Either I'm missing something (quite possible), or [Google](http://www.google.com)'s [calculator](http://www.google.com/intl/en/help/features.html#calculator) converts between joules and calories incorrectly.
According to pretty much everyone else (e.g. [here](http://www.dwlz.com/WWinfo/kilojoules.html)), a calorie is 4.184 kilojoules. [According to Google's calculator](http://www.google.com/search?q=calories+to+kilojoules&btnG=Google+Search), it's 0.004184 kilojoules.
