Last time: [Lewis Road Creamery Chocolate Milk](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/10/03/lewis-road-chocolate-milk/), this time: [Homegrown 100% Pure Orange Juice](https://www.homegrownjuice.co.nz/supermarket-products).

1L plastic bottle, bought at Woolworths, although I've seen it at other supermarkets too. It's in the chilled section, so I doubt you can get it online.

Like the [feijoa smoothie](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/09/28/homegrown-feijoa-smoothie/), it's "cold pressed" and "cold pasteurised". I'm still not sure what that gives me, but I imagine it's better for me.

My [usual juice complaint](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/04/karma-drinks-orange-juice/) applies: I wish it said which variety of oranges was used. It doesn't even say where they're from, beyond "Contains NZ and imported raw cold pasteurised orange juice".

A perfectly reasonable orange juice, but nothing particularly special. It's a little more tart than I would prefer. 7.2/10.
