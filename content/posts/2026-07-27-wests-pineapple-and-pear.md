Last time: [NZ Natural Juice Apple](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/27/nz-juice-apple/), this time: [Wests Pineapple & Pear](https://wests.co.nz/products/pineapple-pear).

350mL plastic bottle. Ordered online - I haven't seen it anywhere else.

No surprise here: 100% flat, even though I'm fairly sure this is meant to be bubbly.

An interesting flavour, though. I don't think I've had pineapple with pear before, and they do pair (sorry) very well together. Unfortunately, it just tastes watered down. An actual pineapple-and-pear juice would probably be great, and a fizzy pineapple-and-pear might be too. This is just meh.

4.8/10.
