A comment [here](http://www.gumboot.co.nz/forums/topic.asp?TOPIC_ID=580) indicates that for loopthrough to work one needs a SCART-SCART cable, and not SCART->component.  That would explain why it doesn't work - and also makes the feature pretty worthless since there must be about 3 TVs in all of New Zealand that have SCART input.
