*(Post theme: [friendly fire (the pool song)](https://music.apple.com/nz/album/friendly-fire-the-pool-song/1779056021?i=1779056212&l=en-GB)).*

This year, I [watched 164 films](https://letterboxd.com/tonyandrewmeyer/). I recommend [Tinā](https://letterboxd.com/film/tina-2024-1/), [KPop Demon Hunters](https://letterboxd.com/film/kpop-demon-hunters/), [The Gorge](https://letterboxd.com/film/the-gorge-2025/), [Swiped](https://letterboxd.com/film/swiped-2025/), [Python: The Documentary](https://letterboxd.com/film/python-the-documentary/), [Girl Haunts Boy](https://letterboxd.com/film/girl-haunts-boy/), and [We Live in Time](https://letterboxd.com/film/we-live-in-time/), and [Sinners](https://letterboxd.com/film/sinners-2025/). A somewhat disappointing year for films for me.

I also [watched 1,259 episodes of TV](https://trakt.tv/users/tonyandrewmeyer). I recommend [Taskmaster](https://trakt.tv/shows/taskmaster), [The Runarounds](https://trakt.tv/shows/the-runarounds), [Murderbot](https://trakt.tv/shows/murderbot), [Department Q](https://trakt.tv/shows/dept-q), and [Shrinking](https://trakt.tv/shows/shrinking). Not a great year for TV, either - a lot of stuff I'm somewhat interested in watching, but nothing that really grabbed me.

On YouTube, I watched a lot more than in previous years. I recommend the [Taskmaster Podcast](https://youtube.com/@taskmaster?si=nrzVR_HDWOql4JTb), the [TalkVille Podcast](https://youtube.com/@talkvillepodcast?si=RHmIXxJa20iONMkk), [Nikki Lu](https://youtube.com/@hellonikkilu?si=rpcs6_t9SgyXaLwM), [Dish Podcast](https://youtube.com/@dishpodcast?si=gQPMKCFSVLHB5L_1), and [Off Menu](https://youtube.com/@offmenupodcast?si=6xaADDqVO1bcxmYH).

I listened to 158 hours of podcasts (this is roughly how long I spent travelling, since that's about the only time I listen to podcasts these days). I recommend [The Morning Shift](https://youknow.media), the [Taskmaster Podcast](https://www.taskmaster.tv/podcast) (again), [Gone By Lunchtime](https://thespinoff.co.nz/podcasts/gone-by-lunchtime), [Juggernaut](https://thespinoff.co.nz/podcasts/juggernaut), [Wait Where Was I](https://linktr.ee/waitwherewasi), and [The Long Play](https://thespinoff.co.nz/podcasts/the-long-play).

I listened to 3,158 songs (around 64,000 minutes) this year, up quite a bit from last year (mostly because I was living alone for most of the year - it is very quiet!). I recommend [Kids Up The Tree](https://music.apple.com/nz/artist/kids-up-the-tree/1722560751), [Cassie Henderson](https://music.apple.com/nz/artist/cassie-henderson/650677095), [fdasavage](https://music.apple.com/nz/artist/fdasavage/1681050958), [aleksiah](https://music.apple.com/nz/artist/aleksiah/1653982021), [Borderline](https://music.apple.com/nz/artist/borderline/1600252390), [Little Green](https://music.apple.com/nz/artist/little-green/1451234268), [Emei](https://music.apple.com/nz/artist/emei/1583968554), [Maddy Low](https://music.apple.com/nz/artist/maddy-low/1653073542), [Fan Club](https://music.apple.com/nz/artist/fan-club/574224562), [Phoebe Grace](https://music.apple.com/nz/artist/phoebe-grace/1795619190), [Brooke Alexx](https://music.apple.com/nz/artist/brooke-alexx/870557676), [Daisy Grenade](https://music.apple.com/nz/artist/daisy-grenade/1611437131), [Chloe Parché](https://music.apple.com/nz/artist/chloe-parch%C3%A9/1554821999), and [LØLØ](https://music.apple.com/nz/artist/l%C3%B8l%C3%B8/1378649732). A really great year for discovering new music.

I read [a lot of stuff online](https://github.com/tonyandrewmeyer/tonyandrewmeyer/blob/main/BLOGROLL.md) (via RSS). I recommend everything I posted to [tonyandrewmeyer.blog](https://tonyandrewmeyer.blog), and in particular: [The Spinoff](https://thespinoff.co.nz), [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net), [Armin Ronacher](https://lucumr.pocoo.org), and [Charity Majors](https://charity.wtf).

I [read 115 books](https://www.goodreads.com/user/show/2229155-tony-meyer) (including paper, ebooks, and audio books) this year (a bit over 47,000 pages). I recommend [The Staff Engineer's Path: A Guide for Individual Contributors Navigating Growth and Change](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/61058107-the-staff-engineer-s-path?ac=1&from_search=true&qid=QkGhTHIiFZ&rank=1), most of [Brandon Sanderson](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/38550.Brandon_Sanderson)'s books, [The Thursday Murder Club](https://www.goodreads.com/series/299267-thursday-murder-club), [The Library Trilogy](https://www.goodreads.com/series/343696-the-library-trilogy), [The Witch Roads](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/217388109-the-witch-roads?from_search=true&from_srp=true&qid=8jM94Ozk55&rank=1), [The Final Architecture](https://www.goodreads.com/series/305076-the-final-architecture), and [Violet and the Velvets: The Case of the Missing Stuff](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/220280451-violet-and-the-velvets?ref=nav_sb_ss_1_21). A good year for enjoyable books, but missing anything that really wowed me, unfortunately. A good [book club](https://www.goodreads.com/review/list/2229155-tony-meyer?ref=nav_mybooks&shelf=j-tolhopf-book-club) year again, and a good year of re-reading some childhood favourites (not all of which stand up).

Happy new year!
