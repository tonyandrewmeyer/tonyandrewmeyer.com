I should also explain the tagline, I suppose.  Some time back, my Dad asked me about the etymology of "blog", and said that his best guess was "bullshit, lies, or gossip".

  
While not [technically correct](http://www.google.com/search?hl=en&q=define%3Ablog&btnG=Search&meta=), it did seem pretty accurate in a lot (not all, of course) of cases.  While hopefully this blog won't contain bullshit, lies or gossip, I liked the line.
