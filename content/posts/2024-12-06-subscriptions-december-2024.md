A while back, I read a post by someone (unfortunately, I don't remember who) about the importance of publicly saying what content you consume and/or pay for. I really like that sentiment, so here's mine, as of December 2024. This doesn't include my 'subscription' to my mortgage, to various insurance plans, and so on :). It also excludes the ones that are really just for Samuel, which I don't really consume.

Charities

Greenpeace. I have mixed feelings about their work, and this started out as one of those awful outside-the-supermarket professional sign-ups. Last year, I planned to cancel it, but was talked into continuing at $5/month.

Heart Research. There's a lot of medical choices for donations - this one is mostly in memory of dad. $30/month. I think this might have also originated as one of those professional sign-ups, but it's one I'm happy to continue with.

Starship. This started as a few one-off donations via various appeals, then I was convinced to make it regular. I very much believe that the government should be 100% funding this work and that they shouldn't be asking for donations at all, but while people vote poorly, I guess this is what has to be done. $50/month.

Media

Netflix - $28/month. It's probably true that the quality of content has gone downhill over the last few years, but it's still probably the streamer I would drop last.

Disney+ - $190/year. When Disney+ was first available in NZ it was terribly short of content - but it was content I did want to see (the MCU shows in particular). When they added Star it became a service that had a lot of nostalgia content and also a lot of stuff that I'd thought about watching back in the linear days, and never got to.

Prime Video - $11/month, plus I'm trying out the (new to NZ) MGM add-on. This is my least favourite of the big streamers, but they have enough shows that I'd like to watch that it's worth keeping on.

(I used to have SkyTV and Neon, and briefly tried Acorn, but have cancelled all those).

Utilities

Google One - additional storage, because it's a convenient and moderately cheap place. $6/month.

Flickr - I've used Flickr since before Samuel was born - I don't upload to it much any more, but still like having it around, and supporting something that isn't Instagram or Facebook. US$80/year.

Microsoft 365 Personal - I used this a lot while working at N-able (to verify behaviour and do demos) and I probably don't need it any more, but haven't gotten around to dropping it. Occasionally, I do have to work with Office files and it is sometimes more convenient to do that with Office than with Google or the Apple apps. $12/month.

GitHub Copilot. I'm not sure whether this really has value or not. I don't interact with it much (not at all on the web, and not at all for the chat interface) - generally it's just providing some improved autocomplete in VS Code. It seemed worth evaluating, though. US$10/month.
