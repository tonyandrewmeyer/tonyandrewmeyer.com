Last time: [Phoenix Organics Apple, Mango, Passionfruit & Orange](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/05/30/phoenix-apple-mango-passionfruit-orange/), this time [Lemon and Te Aroha](https://www.tearohamineralwater.co.nz).

Glass bottle, in a satisfying 330ml size. Another birthday gift hand-delivered from my sister, but I believe they also deliver. I have never seen this before; I don't think I have even heard of the sister water product. Obviously, there's a bit of a play on the name here, against the world-famous-in-New-Zealand drink from a neighbouring town that sounds much the same.

A curious taste. Almost no lemon taste, which is strange given the name. Quite sweet, but not in an overpowering way. It reminds me of something but I can't put my finger on what. Another one I enjoyed more straight from the fridge than over ice.

I would happily drink this again, although I'm unsure if I would order a whole case of it. I hope to see it out and about and on menus, though. 7.75/10.
