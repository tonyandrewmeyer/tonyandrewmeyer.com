Last time: [Picky Feijoa Smoothie](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/09/13/picky-feijoa-smoothie/), this time: [Barrier Buzz Raspberry Lemonade](https://theaoteacompany.co.nz/products/barrier-buzz-raspberry-lemonade-box-of-12-3-75-each).

A 300ml glass bottle, the same as the rest of the range. Bought online, and I still haven't found these anywhere else.

Like all the Barrier Buzz drinks (the [lemonade](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/06/07/barrier-buzz-lemonade/), the [ginger beer](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/06/barrier-buzz-ginger-beer/), and the [cola](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/09/13/barrier-buzz-cola/)), this is really honey water with a hint of another flavour. This is a great choice of flavour, though: the raspberry adds much more than the lemonade does on its own.

There's not much else to say. It has a good amount of fizz, it's the right type of container, it's a reasonable volume, and it's sweet and distinct. The other Buzz drinks are good too (the Cola only just), but I'd choose this one over them.

9.2/10.
