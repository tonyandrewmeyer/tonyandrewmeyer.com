*(Post theme: [Edge of the Earth by The Beaches](https://music.apple.com/nz/album/edge-of-the-earth/1701162686?i=1701163276&l=en-GB))*

Last time: [Benjer Raspberry & Apple](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/23/benjer-raspberry-and-apple/), this time: [NZ Natural Juice Wildberries](https://www.nzjuice.co.nz/collections/nz-natural-juice/products/nz-natural-juice-nfc-wildberry-juice-250ml-12-bottles-pack).

250mL glass bottle, with the same annoying plastic wrap around the top that I've complained about in the [apple](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/27/nz-juice-apple/) and [orange](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/27/nz-juice-orange/) posts. Third time, still annoying, still going straight in the bin.

"Wildberries" is dominated by blueberry and blackcurrant.

This is a well-made juice, and if blueberry is your berry then you'll do very well out of it. But I've been working my way through a run of berry drinks lately, and the conclusion keeps being the same: these aren't the berries I'd prefer to drink. It wasn't bad at all - it's just not what I'd reach for.

5.3/10.
