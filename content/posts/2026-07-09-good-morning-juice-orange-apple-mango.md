Last time: [Wests Cola & Raspberry](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/07/wests-cola-raspberry/), this time: [Good Morning Juice Orange, Apple, and Mango](https://www.nzjuice.co.nz/collections/good-morning-juice/products/good-morning-juice-orange-apple-mango-single-bottle).

2.0L plastic bottle. Ordered online, no issues with delivery. I haven't seen these anywhere else, and I don't think it's available in smaller volumes.

The taste strongly reminded me of Just Juice tropical, and also of the sort of general unlabelled tropical juice you get at a hotel buffet breakfast. Enjoyable - I don't think I would go seeking it out, but I would happily drink it any time it was available.

Interestingly, given my usual gripes about people not specifying the variety of fruit in juice, it does say the oranges are from Gisborne, the apples from Hawke's Bay, and the mango from Australia - which is something, but still not actual varieties.

Overall: 7.7/10.
