Last time: [Good Juju Lemonade](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/09/good-juju-lemonade/), this time: [Picky Hydrate Orange & Mango](https://wearepicky.co.nz/products/orange-mango).

250mL can, found at Woolworths in Wellington - I haven't seen it anywhere else, but perhaps it's new, or perhaps I just wasn't looking properly.

As expected, this is pretty much the [Berry](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/08/picky-hydrate-berry/) again with a different fruit bolted on. There's a clear orange-and-mango taste coming through, which is more than I can say for a lot of flavoured waters, but underneath it is that same ugly no-sugar soda water taste that I couldn't get past last time either.

I really like Picky's juices. I'm much less keen on their water. 4.9/10.
