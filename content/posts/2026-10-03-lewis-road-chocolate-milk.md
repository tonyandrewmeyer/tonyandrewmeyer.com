Last time: [No Ugly Sleep Sparkling Passionfruit](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/10/03/no-ugly-sleep-sparkling-passionfruit/), this time: [Lewis Road Creamery Chocolate Milk](https://lewisroadcreamery.co.nz/products/fresh-chocolate-milk-300ml).

750ml plastic bottle. Obviously, I've had this many times before, going all the way back to the initial crazed release. Was that perhaps the most successful product launch, in terms of marketing, that New Zealand has ever had?

I agree that this is very good, although, to this day, I'm not so convinced that it's the best chocolate milk in the world, as is often claimed (I've said before that it's [great, but not my top pick](https://tonyandrewmeyer.blog/2025/04/04/whatever-the-chocolate-milk-version.html)). It has a very distinct, authentic chocolate flavour, which is the Lewis Road signature.

I'm mostly not looking at price for these drinks, but this is noticeably expensive, and I don't feel it's worth the premium. If it was priced the same as other chocolate milks, then I would likely drink it regularly. However, with things as they are, I only occasionally have one, when the mood strikes. 8.8/10.
