Last time: [Foxton Fizz, Kola](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/05/foxton-fizz-kola/), this time: [Aroha Apple & Pear](https://arohadrinks.co.nz/products/aroha-still-apple-pear-330ml-x-12).

330mL glass bottle, ordered online. I haven't seen this anywhere else, which is odd - it seems like exactly the sort of thing that would do well in cafes and supermarkets.

Apple and pear is an excellent combination, and this is a very good example of it. [Picky's](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/28/picky-apple-and-pear/) is still better, but this is pretty great too - a little lighter on the pear than I would prefer.

What I love most is that the blurb on the bottle calls out the varieties: Cox's Orange for the apple (which I learnt a while back is not an orange at all, but an apple) and Taylors Gold for the pear. More drinks should do that.

Excellent. 8.6/10.
