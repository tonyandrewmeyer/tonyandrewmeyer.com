Last time: [Picky Hydrate Berry](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/08/picky-hydrate-berry/), this time: [Good Juju Pre+ Pro+ Postbiotic Lemonade](https://www.goodjujusoda.com/products/lemonade).

330mL can, found at Woolworths in Wellington - I haven't seen it anywhere else, including back home. No relation to [the Juju I deal with at work](https://canonical.com/juju), obviously.

I can taste the lemon, and I can taste soda water. I can also taste something else, particularly in the aftertaste, that I really don't like - which I assume is the "over 1.3 billion probiotics" making themselves known. Whatever it is, it's the flavour that lingers, and it's not one I want lingering.

This is probably good for me. It's not so good for me that the weird taste is worth suffering.

3.8/10.
