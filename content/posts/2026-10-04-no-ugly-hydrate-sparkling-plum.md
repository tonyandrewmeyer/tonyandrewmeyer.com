Last time: [Homegrown 100% Pure Orange Juice](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/10/04/homegrown-pure-orange/), this time: [No Ugly Hydrate Sparkling Plum](https://nougly.nz/products/hydrate).

250ml can, bought at New World, although you can also get it online.

Like the [Sleep](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/10/03/no-ugly-sleep-sparkling-passionfruit/), I'm not sure how I could really verify that this did anything for me in terms of hydration. I don't see a lot of plum-flavoured drinks, so there's not much to compare this to.

It was nice, but I suspect a simpler plum soft drink or sparkling water would be just as good. 6.2/10.
