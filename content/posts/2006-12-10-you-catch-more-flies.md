Following up on the honeypot successes, I've had two more since [August](http://tonyandrewmeyer.wordpress.com/2006/08/25/yet-more-honeyport-goodness/). [One](http://www.projecthoneypot.org/i_584e1d9be065394ad77196b8299883c7) in early October, and [one](http://www.projecthoneypot.org/i_a1709f8d31c58947adf3aa01856c1622) a couple of days ago, both with the original domain (a .gen.nz) I used to donate.
I had wondered if perhaps the more rapid success for the later donations (.org and .com) meant that spammers were more likely to use the .org/.com addresses, but perhaps that isn't the case (or perhaps it isn't now).
You should still [donate a MX entry](http://www.projecthoneypot.org/refer_a_friend.php) if you can.
