Last time: [Karma Drinks Organic Razza Raspberry Lemonade](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/06/09/razza-raspberry-lemonade/), this time [Pete's Natural Lemonade](https://petesnatural.co.nz/shop/petes-natural-lemonade-12-pack-cans/).

250ml can, although I've had the 300ml bottle too. Both good choices, although 250ml is generally not enough. I've ordered these online before, and I think found them at New World, but I think this one was another birthday gift from my sister, hand delivered.

Overall, it's what you would expect from a lemonade, but very much not on the sweet side (purposefully, I believe). I like a sweeter lemonade (but still with a good twang), so this wouldn't be my first choice. It's good straight from the fridge and with ice; I didn't really have a preference. Cute artwork, and I have a "Pete" doll somewhere.

I'd have it again, but only if I wanted lemonade and ones I preferred were not available. 6/10.
