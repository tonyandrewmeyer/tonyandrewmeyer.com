*(Post theme: [The Only Exception by Paramore](https://music.apple.com/nz/album/the-only-exception/604800411?i=604800419&l=en-GB))*

Last time: [Lewis Road Creamery Caramel and Butterscotch](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/10/03/lewis-road-caramel-butterscotch/), this time: [Clean Collective Energy Forest Berry](https://cleancollective.co.nz/products/energy-forest-berry).

330ml can, bought at New World, although I believe it's available elsewhere too, and you can get it online.

The [pineapple](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/10/03/clean-collective-energy-pineapple/) was a very pleasant surprise, so I went into this one with a lot more hope than I had for that. It mostly lived up to it: forest berry isn't as much my thing as pineapple, but it's still good, and much better than I would expect from a soda water.

Again, it's not trying to be a soft drink. Maybe it is being an energy drink, but it doesn't taste like any energy drink I've had - it's much more like a really great flavoured sparkling water.

I'd have this again too, although (again) I likely wouldn't seek it out. 6.4/10.
