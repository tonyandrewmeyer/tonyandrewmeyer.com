Last time: [Wests Chocolade](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/13/wests-chocolade/), this time: [Benjer Nectarine](https://benjer.co.nz/product/benjer-nectarine/).

275mL glass bottle. Ordered online - I haven't spotted these anywhere else, but they would be a great fit at a cafe.

Like the [apricot juice](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/13/benjer-apricot/), quite viscous, and delicious cold from the fridge. Very strong nectarine flavour - it absolutely tastes like drinking a fresh nectarine. I thoroughly enjoyed this, and I'm not even a huge nectarine fan. I would definitely have this again, although I suspect I couldn't drink a lot of it in one sitting.

Overall: 8.7/10.
