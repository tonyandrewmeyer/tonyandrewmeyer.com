Last time: [Karma Drinks Orange Juice](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/04/karma-drinks-orange-juice/), this time [Wests Raspberry](https://wests.co.nz/products/raspberry).

350ml plastic bottle, as usual for the Wests range.

Once again, very flat - although there were some hints that maybe this is meant to be bubbly. A dull raspberry taste, more of an old-fashioned flavour than a true berry flavour.

I would love to love Wests more, but that's not how it's playing out so far. 3.5/10.
