Last time: [Picky Apple & Pear](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/28/picky-apple-and-pear/), this time: [Picky 100% JAZZ Apple](https://wearepicky.co.nz/products/100-jazz-apple-juice).

800mL (great) plastic (not great) bottle.

Finally, someone that not only specifies the apple variety but also makes it the most prominent thing on the bottle. JAZZ is the variety of apple I eat most - probably because it was Sam's favourite and I have never really branched out - and this very clearly tastes like drinking one of those.

There's really nothing to complain about here: it is exactly what you would want from apple juice. But, still, it *is* apple juice, and that's almost by definition a bit uninteresting. Great cold, and great with ice.

Exactly what it says on the bottle. 7.9/10.
