*(Post theme: [Hardly There by Keeley Shade](https://music.apple.com/nz/album/hardly-there/1797675094?i=1797675098&l=en-GB))*

Last time: [Good Morning Juice Apple & Blueberry](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/27/good-morning-juice-apple-blueberry/), this time: [NZ Natural Juice Orange](https://www.nzjuice.co.nz/collections/nz-natural-juice/products/nz-natural-juice-nfc-orange-juice-250ml-12-bottles-pack).

250mL glass bottle, with the same annoying and disappointing plastic wrapper around the top that I complained about in the [apple](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/27/nz-juice-apple/) post. It hasn't got any easier to remove in the meantime.

Unsurprisingly, and yet still disappointingly, there's no information about the type of orange.

Orange is one of the best juices, in many forms. This one is smooth rather than pulpy, and not overly sweet, but it's also not particularly distinctive. It's good - it's just good in the way that most orange juice is good.

7.1/10.
