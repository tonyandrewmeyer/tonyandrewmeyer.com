*(Post theme: [I Really Like You by Little Green](https://music.apple.com/nz/album/i-really-like-you/1807628950?i=1807628959&l=en-GB))*

Last time: [Pete's Natural Lemonade](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/06/14/petes-natural-lemonade/), this time [Lewis Road Creamery Boysenberry Cheesecake](https://lewisroadcreamery.co.nz/products/boysenberry-cheesecake).

750ml plastic bottle, although it's available in the little 300ml form as well. 300ml is not terrible, but, given you know Lewis Road will be good, you might as well just go for the large. I picked this up at New World, and presumably it will be available from Woolworths too, although they always lag several weeks before new products are available. Likely available from service stations as well. I don't generally see milk available at cafes, which is a shame, really. Online ordering and delivery via rural post seems impractical here.

The thing I love about Lewis Road is that the milk tastes like the flavour it's named after. Not a candy version, not hints, not some extinct variety, but the real thing. Their strawberry milk is probably my favourite milk of all time (their [chocolate is great, but not my top pick](https://tonyandrewmeyer.blog/2025/04/04/whatever-the-chocolate-milk-version.html)). The boysenberry cheesecake is no different: this isn't a boysenberry flavour, it's definitely the boysenberry flavour on a cheesecake, with the cheesecake aspect coming through strongly. I'm not a big fan of cheesecake, but if I were, this could be in my top 3 milks, I think.

Overall, it's everything you'd expect from Lewis Road Creamery. Everyone should try all their flavours. Straight from the fridge -- I guess ice would work but I rarely bother with milk (and you don't want water diluting it).

I'd have it again, but realistically only if strawberry or the other Lewis Road ones I prefer were not available. 7.5/10.
