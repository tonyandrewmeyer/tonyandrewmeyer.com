Last time: [Clean Collective Energy Peach and Nectarine](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/10/16/clean-collective-energy-peach-nectarine/), this time: [Skull Choc](https://skullmilk.nz/).

425ml plastic bottle, bought at a Z service station - I haven't seen it anywhere else. I assume this is pretty new based on the very placeholder website.

This tastes like a homemade chocolate milkshake, which makes it unlike most of the other chocolate milks around. Like lots of things these days it calls out the amount of protein it has, but I don't pay enough attention to notice, or really care.

Nice, but not particularly special. 7.3/10.
