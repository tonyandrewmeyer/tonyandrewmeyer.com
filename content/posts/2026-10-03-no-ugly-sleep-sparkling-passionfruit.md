Last time: [Clean Collective Energy Forest Berry](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/10/03/clean-collective-energy-forest-berry/), this time: [No Ugly Sleep Sparkling Passionfruit](https://nougly.nz/products/sleep).

250ml can, bought at New World, although you can also get it online.

I never remember that I don't like passionfruit. Maybe it's because when it's mixed into some sort of "tropical" (or other) medley it's quite fine. On its own, though, it's definitely not my thing, so it's no surprise that this isn't either.

I assume this is good for me, but that's not enough to make up for the taste. I didn't notice any difference in my sleep, but am I meant to drink this before bed every night or something? That seems unrealistic. 4.2/10.
