Last time: [Wakachangi Quite Nice Fizz Cola](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/05/wakachangi-quite-nice-fizz-cola/), this time: [Foxton Fizz, Orange](https://foxtonfizz.com/products/orange).

250mL glass bottle, ordered online, although I expect it's in New World too - I just don't see the orange one on the shelf very often.

Same as with the lime, this is an orange in the Fanta sense rather than anything that's trying to taste honestly of the fruit. Sweet, bright, a bit artificial - which is fine, as long as that's what you're after, and here I mostly was.

It's Foxton Fizz, so the basics are all in place: the glass bottle, the mousse, the nostalgia I still can't source. That carries it a fair way on its own.

Not my favourite of the bunch, though. There are several in the range I'd pick ahead of it, and it doesn't have the small extra something that those ones do.

5.8/10.
