Last time: [Six Barrel Lemonade](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/27/six-barrel-lemonade/), this time: [NZ Natural Juice Gold Peach](https://www.nzjuice.co.nz/collections/nz-natural-juice/products/nz-natural-juice-nfc-gold-peach-juice-250ml-12-bottles-pack).

250mL glass bottle, ordered online - I've not seen these anywhere else. Same plastic wrapper in the lid as [every](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/27/nz-juice-apple/) [other](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/27/nz-juice-orange/) [one](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/23/nz-juice-wildberries/) of theirs, and it's no less annoying the fourth time around.

I've never loved peaches. A nectarine is alright, but even that's not something I'd go out of my way for, so a peach juice was never likely to be a winner here, and it wasn't. This is very much peach: if you like peaches, I'd guess this is a perfectly good example of one, made as carefully as the rest of the range. That's about the most useful thing I can tell you, because the flavour is the thing I don't want, and there's nothing else going on to distract me from it.

Not for me, but not really the juice's fault. 4.4/10.
