Last time: [Benjer Apricot](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/13/benjer-apricot/), this time: [Wests Chocolade](https://wests.co.nz/products/chocolade).

330mL plastic bottle. Ordered online - I haven't seen it anywhere else.

I keep hoping to love one of these Wests sodas, and I keep failing. Again, so little fizz as to be almost non-existent. Chocolate is an interesting choice for a soda, so I was extra-hopeful here, but this tasted like a flat Coke knock-off, with maybe a hint of chocolate - although I wonder if that was entirely psychosomatic.

3.5/10.
