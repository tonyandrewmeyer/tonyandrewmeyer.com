Last time: [Barrier Buzz Lemonade](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/06/07/barrier-buzz-lemonade/), this time [Karma Drinks Organic Razza Raspberry Lemonade](https://karmadrinks.co.nz/products/razza-raspberry-lemonade).

330ml can. These are pretty widely available. I don't remember where I picked this up, maybe the supermarket?

Tastes exactly as described: raspberry and lemon. I don't think this is a combination I particularly enjoy, but it's nice enough. Good with ice or straight from the fridge.

I'd drink it again, if Karma was all that was available (not uncommon in certain types of place). 6.5/10.
