Last time: [Wests Raspberry](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/04/wests-raspberry/), this time [Barrier Buzz Ginger Beer](https://theaoteacompany.co.nz/products/barrier-buzz-ginger-beer).

A 300ml glass bottle, matching the [lemonade](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/06/07/barrier-buzz-lemonade/). I haven't seen this for sale anywhere in person - I ordered online directly, with no issues.

Much like the lemonade, this is really a sparkling honey with a hint of ginger, rather than what you might expect from a ginger beer. The honey is out in front, and the ginger sits quietly behind it - present, but not the star of the show. Still very good, and I'd happily drink it again, but if you're after a proper ginger-forward ginger beer this isn't going to scratch that itch.

I'd definitely recommend it, as long as you're on board with honey. 8.5/10.
