Last time: [Karma Drinks Organic Karma Cola](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/08/22/karma-drinks-karma-cola/), this time: [Benjer Cherry & Apple](https://benjer.co.nz/product/benjer-cherry-apple/).

275mL glass bottle, ordered online.

I love cherries. They're a top-three fruit, at least. So it's genuinely strange to me that I have never found a cherry juice I like, and this one doesn't break the run.

The taste of cherries is definitely here, and yet in a drink I still don't love it. Cherry 100% works for me when I'm eating it and not when I'm drinking it.

The apple helps. A good amount of the taste is apple, and that's the part I keep coming back for, in the same way as the [Feijoa & Apple](https://tonyandrewmeyer.com/2026/07/15/benjer-feijoa-and-apple/) and the rest of the Benjer apple blends. But it's still not right for me.

5.3/10. Not the juice's fault, I suspect.
